# NuriVoyage Stage 10 Invoices Payments and Balances

Stage 10 adds local customer invoicing and receipt tracking. Invoices have an invoice number, issue status, amount, paid amount and balance snapshot. Payments have a payment number, type (deposit, payment, refund or credit), allocation amount, reference and customer invoice link. These are synthetic local records; posted accounting integrations, banking and AWS remain out of scope.
